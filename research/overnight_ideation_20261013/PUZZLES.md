## Additional constraints and puzzles

- **P9 — reliability headroom (A):** in `outputs/qwen35_transfer_20261012/task_rates.csv`, all114 baseline tasks have at least1/24 success. The73 inconsistently solved tasks hold19.164 aggregate points of residual error after training; the41 base-perfect tasks lose only.256 aggregate points. Recovering +2 beyond current recipe requires about10.3% of remaining errors, not merely preserving the already-perfect group. This is descriptive headroom, not reachability evidence. Grouping on baseline outcome has regression-to-mean bias.
- **P10 — protocol sensitivity (M):** Qwen3.5 changes from about53 to78 under a joint rendering/sampling repair, dwarfing training effects. Mechanistic claims must preserve the actual deployed information state. Current-step reasoning can span several tool exchanges before the next real user message.
- **P11 — dense local supervision vs sparse causal leverage (M/H):** token-mean reduction is verified; a universal90% reasoning-token share is not yet verified for every current cache. Much loss may concern useful computation rather than external action, but “reweight actions” is neither a diagnosis nor a novel algorithm.
- **P12 — format is useful and dangerous (M/H):** untrained special-token denominators produce very large shifts in the legacy Base→2507 pair, and zeroing those terms hurt; amplifying an external agentic delta harms parallel-call/stop behavior. Simple format removal is not justified by format contamination alone.

## Steelman the ceiling
The anchors may contain only a small amount of useful complementary competence for these strong students. Current DOPD may already extract most transferable information at a safe policy distance; greater movement imports donor weaknesses, and alternative credit schemes may only reshape a weak surrogate. There is no verified stronger teacher rescue or reliable per-state ranking oracle. A finalist must add an identifiable mechanism and explain why it is learnable at this budget; a constructed toy or residual benchmark error does not reject this null.

## Retrodiction discipline
“Explained” requires a causal mechanism that predicts the direction under stated assumptions. “Compatible/neutral” means the proposal does not explain the observation. Retrospective plausibility is not confirmation. A proposal need not fit numerical noise or every unrelated benchmark protocol, and should not make unfalsifiable explanations that can predict either direction after seeing the result.
